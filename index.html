StickMindR8 Stats

A personal tool for the StickMindR8 channel.

StickMindR8 Stats is a small private tool used by one person: the owner of the StickMindR8 YouTube channel, which publishes animated stickman stories and short videos.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's own YouTube channel statistics (views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers, top videos) so the owner can see what works and improve future videos.
  • May help the owner manage their own uploads: video titles, descriptions, tags, playlists and thumbnails.

Who it is for

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, has no other users and does not collect data from viewers.
