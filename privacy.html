Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

StickMindR8 Stats ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the StickMindR8 YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

After the channel owner signs in with Google and gives permission, the app can access data from their own YouTube account only:

  • Basic channel information and the list of the owner's videos and playlists.
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel (for example views, watch time, retention, subscribers and traffic sources).
  • If the owner chooses to enable it, the ability to upload videos and edit the metadata (title, description, tags, playlist, thumbnail) of the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the channel owner their own statistics, to help them improve their own content, and to manage their own videos at their request. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

The app has no public users and no public database. Channel data is processed on the owner's own devices or accounts and is kept only as long as needed to analyze it. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties. Where an AI assistant is used by the owner to analyze the statistics, the data is shared only with that assistant, at the owner's request, for the owner's own analysis.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account permissions. You can also ask us to delete any stored data by writing to revolverius08@gmail.com. YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions: revolverius08@gmail.com.
